# Mac Screen Recorder Decision Tree

Observed: 2026-09-01

> Disclosure: Mac Creator Lab is run by the team behind ScreenSage Pro. ScreenSage Pro appears here as a disclosed vendor product, not as an independent recommendation.

Choose by the work that remains **after** recording, not by feature-count alone.

- Raw capture is enough → start with QuickTime Player or OBS Studio.
- Screenshots are as important as recordings → CleanShot X is the natural category fit.
- Local-first / open-source-oriented workflow → look at Cap.
- Hosted async team communication is the main job → Loom.
- Deeper traditional editing / training production → Camtasia.
- Presentation-first Mac product demos and tutorials → compare Screen Studio and ScreenSage Pro.

The point of this tree is not to declare one universal winner. Different recorders optimize for different post-capture jobs.
